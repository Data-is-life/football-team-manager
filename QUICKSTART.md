# Quick start

[Project overview](README.md) · [Developer setup](docs/development.md)

Build your franchise one season at a time. You make roster decisions; the game
simulates the action and shows the final scores and player stats.

## Your first season

1. **Choose a team.** A new career takes over before Week 17. The first 16 games
   are inherited history; your personal GM record starts at zero.
2. **Review the roster.** Current measures ability now; potential is a possible
   ceiling. Starters are chosen automatically by current rating. Bench depth
   contributes to team ratings, so keep useful backups.
3. **Simulate the final week.** Check Game Stats, Schedule, Standings, and Rankings.
   Later seasons begin at Week 1 and run for 17 games.
4. **Finish the playoffs.** Select **View playoffs** and advance all four rounds,
   even if your team is eliminated or misses qualification. Fourteen teams qualify;
   the top two seeds receive first-round byes.

## Build the next roster

1. **Draft six players.** Compare current and potential ratings with your roster
   needs. New prospects enter at ages 21–23. In new careers, prospects remaining after round one start at no more than 70 current rating, including UDFAs; their potential is preserved.
2. **Sign your own players.** Renew or release expiring veterans, and sign or
   release each drafted rookie. Active contracts continue automatically. Resolve
   cap issues before opening the market.
3. **Use free agency.** Sign at fixed asking prices or end the market. There are
   no waves, passes, or automatic discounts. CPU teams also compete for players.
   The pool contains current-class UDFAs, players teams let go, and unsigned
   players returning for their one allowed carryover season.
4. **Review payroll and Trades.** Keep 31–38 players, satisfy position minimums,
   and fit the 60% payroll floor and salary cap. Use **Meet payroll minimum** if
   needed. Preview 1-for-1 trades and confirm accepted offers. Receiving teams
   assume the full remaining guarantee; current-year rookies and new free-agent
   signings cannot be traded.
5. **Start the season.** Choose **Finish trading and start season**. Aging and
   development run automatically once, with no training-camp step. Rookie outcomes
   are 25% boom, 25% bust, and 50% regular development.

Contracts are fully guaranteed: releasing a signed player leaves the remaining
guarantee as dead cap. Check the release confirmation before committing.

## Keep your job and your progress

- Reach the playoffs at least once in your first four full seasons (Seasons 2–5
  for a new late-start career). The introductory season is a grace period.
- Each full season earns only its highest extension: championship +5 years,
  playoff appearance +2, or a winning record +1. Follow owner feedback in GM Records.
- Progress autosaves in the same browser and site address. Refresh to resume;
  starting a new career replaces the existing save after loading successfully.

Use **How to Play** for in-game guidance. See the
[rules reference](docs/rules-reference.md) for formulas and older-career differences.

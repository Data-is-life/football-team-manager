Your GM contract

Contract rules

Make the playoffs at least once in your first four seasons or lose the job, even if winning records earn extensions. Each season earns only the highest reward: championship +5 years; otherwise playoffs +2; otherwise a winning record (more wins than losses) +1; otherwise no extension. Extensions add to your remaining contract. After the opening deadline, an expired contract ends your career.
